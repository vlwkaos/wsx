The reader sees one screen. Write for that screen:

- Put the answer, the result or the decision first.
- If a term, a name or a fact is not verified, say so on the first line: `Assuming: ...`.
- Show structure as structure. Steps, a flow, parts and how they relate go in a mermaid diagram; a comparison goes in a table. Use prose only for what has no shape.
- Say each fact once. Do not restate the question, narrate what you did or recap at the end.
- Keep a reply to about one phone screen (25 short lines). Detail the reader may need goes in a file or a page, and the reply links it.
- Write at full length only for what the reader asked for, for errors and for what the reader needs to act safely.
