# Terminal capture checklist

Use this checklist before sharing or publishing an artifact. For local-only inspection, apply the relevant capture and cleanup items.

## Before capture

- [ ] Purpose, artifact type, and minimum scope are clear.
- [ ] Destination and audience are explicit.
- [ ] Human consent exists for consequential content or disclosure.
- [ ] Credential entry, notifications, unrelated panes, and verbose tracing are excluded.
- [ ] Output path is current-run-owned local scratch or an explicit durable path.

## Before disclosure

- [ ] The sanitized copy, not the private original, is selected.
- [ ] Visible content excludes secrets, identity details, private paths, hosts, repository names, messages, customer data, and unrelated history.
- [ ] Window titles, prompts, timestamps, QR codes, and metadata were checked.
- [ ] Every recording interval that can reveal typed or changing content was reviewed.
- [ ] Opaque redaction or cropping makes removed content unrecoverable.
- [ ] The approved audience and destination match the actual disclosure.

## Afterward

- [ ] The delivered artifact was reopened and verified.
- [ ] Current-run originals and scratch were removed unless retention was requested.
- [ ] Retained paths and reasons were reported.
- [ ] Any possible secret exposure was reported by category without repeating its value.
